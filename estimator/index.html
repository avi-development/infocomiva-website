Frequently asked questions

How much does a mobile app cost to build in India in 2026?

Indian software agencies in 2026 typically charge ₹3-12L for a standard iOS + Android mobile app (React Native or Flutter) including auth, payments, push, and store submission. Freelancer pools quote lower (often ₹1-5L) with variable quality. Big-name IT services charge 4-8x more for the same scope due to overhead. Complexity (offline-first, AI features, multi-tenant backend) scales the number 2-4x.

How much does custom SaaS development cost in India?

A production-grade multi-tenant SaaS build costs ₹8-30L at a mid-market Indian agency, ₹15L-1Cr at a tier-1 IT services firm, and USD 80k-300k at a US/UK agency. Pricing depends primarily on whether multi-tenancy is hybrid (shared app, isolated DBs — most common) or fully shared, and on the number of customer-facing modules.

Are freelancers cheaper than agencies in India?

Freelancers win on hourly cost — typical Upwork rates are ₹800-3000/hr against ₹1500-4000/hr at mid-market agencies. But freelancer projects often need more rounds, lack code ownership, and have no production-grade support after launch. For projects beyond a single landing page or one-off script, the total cost-to-finished-product is often higher with freelancers, not lower.

Why are big IT services firms 4-8x more expensive than mid-market agencies?

Tier-1 IT services firms carry enterprise sales teams, multi-layer project management hierarchies, audit overhead, and procurement-friendly billing structures. The engineering work isn’t 4-8x harder — the markup pays for the overhead the agency model carries. Mid-market agencies skip most of that layer and price closer to the actual delivery cost.

How accurate are these estimates?

Estimators give realistic ranges, not binding quotes. Real numbers depend on scope detail that only a discovery call can surface — integrations, data volumes, compliance, design ambition, edge cases.
