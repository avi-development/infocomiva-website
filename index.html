Straight talk

When you should not hire us.

Five scenarios where we’re the wrong choice. We’ll tell you on the discovery call — but you can save the call by reading this now.

01
You need a SOC2 / PCI / HIPAA audit firm.

We’ll build compliance-friendly architecture, but the audit itself needs a certified firm. We’ll introduce you to one we’ve worked with.

02
You want the cheapest possible quote.

We’re mid-market — not freelancer-cheap, not enterprise-extortion. If price is the only axis, Upwork will undercut us. Fine.

03
You want hourly billing with open scope.

We do fixed scope, fixed price. If you’d rather pay-as-you-go on an open brief, a contractor model fits better — and we’ll still help you scope it.

04
You need embedded firmware or chip-level work.

Outside our depth. We’ll happily integrate with your firmware team, but we’re not the ones writing it.

05
You want us to be a body-shop.

We take outcome ownership, not headcount tickets. If “3 devs for 6 months, you manage them” is the ask, a staff-aug shop is the right call.
