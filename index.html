Why us

You’ve been burned by an agency before. Here’s why this is different.

Four objections every full-stack partner deserves to hear. Straight answers, with the receipts — we built and still operate TraxnCargo on the same stack, the same team, the same terms we’re offering you.

If you’re worried about
Jack of all trades, master of none.

Eight practice areas, eight senior owners — not one generalist stretched eight ways. Each lead has shipped 5+ production systems in their domain. Where we don’t have depth (e.g. specialized SEO data-science, embedded firmware), we say so up front and bring in a vetted partner. We don’t fake expertise to win the brief.

If you’re worried about
A rigid framework that punishes pivots.

“Discover · Design · Ship · Support” gets us out of the gate. After that, two-week iterations and a contract clause: any pivot inside the original budget is absorbed, not change-ordered. Three of our last five projects pivoted mid-build. We rebuilt scope, kept the price.

If you’re worried about
Vendor lock-in & single point of failure.

Your code lives in your GitHub org from day one. Your domain stays on your registrar. Your Vercel / AWS / Cloudflare bill goes to you directly. Architecture docs, runbooks, env reference — handed off at every milestone. If you want to take it in-house in month four, the only thing you stop paying is us.

If you’re worried about
Surprise invoices & agency-premium pricing.

Written scope and fixed price before discovery ends. Milestone billing, no change-order trap. Post-launch is an optional retainer at a published hourly rate — never a quote-per-ticket black box. If we under-quote due to our own miscalculation, we eat the difference. That clause is in the MSA.
